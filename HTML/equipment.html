IMMERSION BREWING

French Press

A classic brewer that produces rich, full-bodied coffee with simple preparation.

Best for

Morning coffee and shared brews.

How to use

Add coarse-ground coffee and hot water. Stir gently and steep for about four minutes. Press the plunger down slowly and pour the coffee.

ESPRESSO

Espresso Machine

Designed for concentrated espresso and café-style drinks at home.

Best for

Espresso, cappuccino and latte.

How to use

Fill the water reservoir and allow the machine to heat up. Add ground coffee to the portafilter and tamp evenly. Brew according to the manufacturer's instructions, then clean the equipment after use.

FILTER BREWING

Pour Over Dripper

A controlled brewing method that can highlight delicate coffee flavours.

Best for

Light and medium roast coffees.

How to use

Place a paper filter in the dripper and rinse it with hot water. Add medium-fine ground coffee. Pour a little hot water over the grounds, wait about 30 seconds, then continue pouring in circular movements.

£29.00

A consistent grinder that gives you greater control over coffee preparation.

Best for

Multiple brewing methods.

How to use

Add coffee beans to the hopper. Select a grind setting appropriate for your brewing method. Grind the beans and follow the manufacturer's instructions for cleaning.

£99.00

PRECISION BREWING

Digital Coffee Scale

Helps measure coffee and water so your favourite brew can be repeated more consistently.

Best for

Pour-over and manual brewing.

How to use

Place the scale on a flat surface and switch it on. Put your container on the scale and press tare to reset the weight. Measure your coffee and water before brewing.

£42.00

STOVETOP BREWING

Moka Pot

A traditional stovetop brewer for bold, concentrated coffee.

Best for

Strong coffee and milk-based drinks.

How to use

Fill the lower chamber with water up to the safety valve. Add ground coffee to the filter basket without pressing it down. Assemble the pot and heat it according to its instructions.

£38.00

PRESSURE BREWING

AeroPress

A compact brewer that combines immersion and pressure for a smooth cup.

Best for

Travel and quick manual brewing.

How to use

Place a filter in the cap and attach it to the chamber. Add ground coffee and hot water, then stir gently. Steep briefly and press slowly over a sturdy cup.

£45.00

WATER CONTROL

Gooseneck Kettle

A precision-pouring kettle designed for controlled manual brewing.

Best for

Pour-over and slow-pour methods.

How to use

Fill the kettle within its marked limits and heat the water. Hold the handle securely and pour slowly over the coffee grounds. Take care when handling hot water.

£70.00

MILK PREPARATION

Electric Milk Frother

Create smooth, warm foam for café-style drinks at home.

Best for

Latte, cappuccino and mocha drinks.

How to use

Add milk up to the indicated fill line, close the lid and select the appropriate setting. Wait for the cycle to finish before pouring. Keep the electrical base dry.

COFFEE STORAGE

Coffee Storage Canister

A practical container for protecting roasted coffee between brews.

Best for

Freshness and organised storage.

How to use

A compact accessory for disposing of used espresso grounds more conveniently.

Best for

Espresso stations and home cafés.

How to use

Siphon Brewer

A distinctive brewing method that creates a refined specialty coffee experience.

Best for

Specialty coffee and tasting sessions.

How to use

Assemble the brewer according to its instructions. Heat the water until it rises into the upper chamber, add coffee and stir. Remove the heat when brewing is complete and allow the coffee to drain. Handle hot glass carefully.
